# La Carihuela Chica design system

Mediterranean restaurant editorial identity. Product photography leads; navy, ivory and the existing warm gold carry the brand.

- Typography: Cormorant Garamond regular for headings; Manrope for body, navigation, controls and numeric information. No decorative italics or monospace.
- Spacing: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 96px. Related content shares a spacing group. Desktop sections use 96px; mobile 64px.
- Colors: navy #153749, paper #fcfbf8, muted ink #52636b, gold #c6a866. Gold buttons always use dark ink text. Dark sections use light supporting text.
- Controls: 48px primary buttons, visible focus states, rectangular editorial controls. Lucide icons at consistent 1.6 stroke width.
- Numbers: tabular lining figures for prices, ratings and counters.
- Corners: photographic layouts and buttons are square. When a padded rounded container is necessary, outer radius 8px, padding 8px, inner radius max(0, outer minus padding).
- Content: specific dishes, actual location and verified reviews. No filler badges, decorative coordinates or repeated category labels.
- Motion: purposeful entrance, photo crossfade and gallery transitions. Respect reduced motion. Never hijack scrolling.
